# Data Analytics Course Launch — Requirements Engineering

**CBAP training project · Business Analysis**

Requirements gathering and strategy for an EdTech company launching a new Data Analytics course that gives learners real-world, industry-style project experience.

## What this project covers

**1. Business analysis planning**
- Planned the business process analysis and stakeholder engagement approach
- Assessed the existing business approach before defining the new course requirements
- Prepared for elicitation across eight stakeholder groups: content team, subject matter experts, technical team, IT team, talent acquisition (trainers), program managers, learners, and operations

**2. Learner platform (LMS) requirements**
- Live session hub: upcoming sessions (streamed on Teams), archived recordings with password-protected access
- Self-paced learning: module-wise content and video recordings, interactive PDFs, quizzes and assignments per module
- Assessment engine: instant results for objective exams, 24-hour turnaround for subjective exams, retake policy, and certification criteria (85% module completion, 60% industry-readiness exam, 80% live-session attendance, hackathon participation)
- Support model: academic queries routed directly to SMEs (24h TAT), 24/7 technical and student support, TA-led 1:1 doubt sessions, and a chatbot recommendation

**3. Course content requirements**
- Industry-relevant modules delivered by industry experts: MS Excel, SQL, Python, data visualization, Power BI, Tableau
- Hands-on exercises preparing students for real-world scenarios and certifications

**4. Requirements table**
- Structured requirements matrix mapping each stakeholder to their user journey, roles, business drivers, benefits, needs, wants, and non-functional requirements
